Scope, sources and date conventions

This is a country-by-country head-of-state research register, not a list of every person who exercised political power. It includes monarchs, presidents, recorded acting holders, military and collective heads, plus selected parallel authorities and executive tracks. It does not comprehensively include all prime ministers, party chiefs, colonial governors, insurgent commanders, subnational rulers or members of every council.

The country directory uses the OIC’s 57-member set as a scope checklist. It does not imply that all were independent states or OIC members throughout 1926–2026. Current country names index predecessor histories. North and South Yemen remain separate tracks inside Yemen’s single row. Before 1971 the UAE had no federal president; its constituent emirates are not individually expanded in this edition. Oman’s separate inland imamate is not expanded here.

Blank or hatched time does not mean that no authority existed. It may denote colonial administration, a predecessor political arrangement, a vacancy, or a period outside the offices covered. Each country’s coverage note is available through “Coverage note”. No missing officeholder is invented to fill a visual gap.

Source dates are retained as Gregorian dates. “End / transfer” normally marks the handover boundary, rather than a calculation of the final full day served. Same-day terms are retained. Some accessions have different proclamation, swearing-in, effective-control or recognition dates; recorded distinctions appear in the notes. Adjacent same-person terms are sometimes combined. “Current” means carried through the fixed cutoff, not beyond it.

The Hijri year axis is a calculated civil/tabular conversion, not a claim about local historical moon sightings. Plot geometry uses elapsed days, so overlap is not distorted by the difference between Hijri and Gregorian year lengths.

Historical rosters are drawn principally from Rulers.org and cited national succession lists, with selected official and contemporary reporting for transfers and disputed cases. Every tenure has a source URL. This is a research edition: remaining date discrepancies, acting substitutions and contested-office coverage require archival review before encyclopedia publication.
