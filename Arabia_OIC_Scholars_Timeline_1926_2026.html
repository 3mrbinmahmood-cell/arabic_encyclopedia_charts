Color key: sect, creed, school and order are different categories

The detailed color is one principal reference affiliation for navigation, not an exhaustive definition of a person. Deobandi and Barelvi are scholarly movements; Ashʿari and Maturidi are distinct theological schools; Sufi orders can overlap those labels and a legal madhhab. Choose Broad branch only to avoid treating those overlapping Sunni categories as separate branches.

A label is not inferred from nationality, a name, a university attended, or a government appointment. Mature affiliation may differ from childhood background. Qualifications appear in the individual profile. The color categories neither validate nor reject theological claims.

Coverage, dates and source limitations

Selected edition. This directory retains the 57-country structure from the companion atlases, with India and Bosnia as explicitly non-OIC supplementary chapters. There are selected profiles in 35 rows; the other rows are unfilled research areas, not countries without scholars. Modern rows do not imply historic independence, membership or borders throughout the century. One principal row is used per person, including people who worked across borders.

Inclusion. Jurists, Quran and hadith scholars, prominent religious teachers, national and regional muftis, heads of religious institutions and selected religious-political writers. This is not a ranking of scholarship or a complete list of all major scholars. It does not evaluate political legitimacy.

Opposition. Dated criticism, exile, detention and state-dispute records are described individually. Detention alone does not establish guilt or a particular political program. A peaceful critic, an opposition-appointed mufti, and a religious-political officeholder are not interchangeable categories. No scholar is presumed an opponent merely because they held no state post.

Precision and endpoints. Year and month dates are expanded only for geometry. Labels preserve original precision. A light band ends at death or the last dated observation included in this edition; later intervals are dotted and unverified. Some dated observations are old. The chart’s 30 September 2026 cutoff is not a claim that every current office was verified on that date. Birth-year disagreements are retained.

Calendars. Horizontal position follows elapsed Gregorian days; Hijri ticks use the calculated civil Islamic calendar inherited from the companion atlas, not historical local moon sightings. The workbook gives separate annual CE and AH grids. This timeline opens 8 January 1926.

Source quality. A reference-based research edition: many biographical dates and classifications rely on linked encyclopedia articles; selected appointments and events also use institutional sources and dated reporting. Not all underlying appointment decrees or original biographical sources were inspected. A source link is not a claim of primary-source corroboration. Sect classifications, dates and political episodes should be checked against original texts before publication as an archival encyclopedia.
