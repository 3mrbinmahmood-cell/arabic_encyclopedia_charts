Scope, dating and source notes

Coverage. The inherited 57-country OIC directory is used for geographical navigation; it does not imply membership, independent statehood or current borders throughout the century. Arabia is listed first. Historical territories are mapped to modern rows for comparison, not as a sovereignty determination. Major external powers are named inside entries even when they have no row.

Selection. This research edition prioritizes major wars, independence struggles, sustained insurgencies and selected consequential armed crises. Short sieges, interventions and armed revolutionary episodes are explicitly marked ◇. It is not a comprehensive conflict dataset or a legal classification under international humanitarian law. Colours describe conflict context, not legitimacy or political approval.

One ID, several countries. A war may appear in several rows. Each bar opens the same shared entry; its country placement can have a different start, end, type and role. Selected external participation does not mean the participant’s whole territory was a battlefield. Diplomatic sympathy, arms sales and membership of a coalition alone are not systematically charted.

Periods and uncertainty. Where sources give only a year or month, that whole unit is used for geometry. Exact-looking geometry bounds in exports are not newly verified historical dates. The displayed date labels and precision field preserve this distinction. Conflicts predating 8 January 1926 are clipped. Peace treaties, withdrawals, independence and the end of fighting can occur on different dates: the notes identify the endpoint used.

Current conflicts. Dated reporting or monitoring gives a status-as-of date. A reference-page status check is explicitly identified and is not equivalent to a separate field assessment. The chart cutoff does not prove a conflict continued at the same intensity to that day. Dotted continuation after the last dated update means later status was not established here; striped bars do not claim a settled outcome.

Calendars. The horizontal position is proportional to Gregorian elapsed days. Hijri year ticks use a calculated civil Islamic calendar; they are not historical local moon-sighting determinations. Date inputs use CE. The workbook includes separate annual CE and AH grids.

Sources. Each entry links the historical references or reporting used for its dates, description and outcome. These include reference encyclopedias, museums, institutional histories, CFR, ACLED and dated journalism. This is a source-linked research edition, not an archival critical edition. Source titles may encompass broader phases than the selected bar.

Reading short events. A minimum-width marker is used for accessibility; it exaggerates only the on-screen width of very short events. Read the tooltip or detail dates for duration. Record totals count selected entries, including umbrella conflicts and subcampaigns—not independent wars or cumulative war-years.
